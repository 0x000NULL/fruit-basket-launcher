  No. 1   Pomegranate   PS2 emulator
  No. 2   Strawberry    GBA emulator
  No. 3   Grenadine     PS2 recomp pipeline (Pomegranate, pressed)
  No. 4   Fig           PS1 emulator

  No. 7   Crabapple     NES emulator
  No. 8   Mulberry      SNES emulator
  No. 9   Olive         Game Boy and Game Boy Color emulator

RULES
  1  One fruit for each console, chosen for something true about the machine.
  2  Ink draws the fruit. Red is only ever its seeds: points, never a fill.
  3  A console whose dots you can see gets a pixel fruit, at whole-number
     scales. A console drawn at window resolution gets a sharp one.
  4  The mark shows only where no game is: the app icon, the site, an empty library.
  5  Console names stay out of the marks. The fruit is the name of the program.
  6  A recompiler is a preserve, made from its console's fruit.
