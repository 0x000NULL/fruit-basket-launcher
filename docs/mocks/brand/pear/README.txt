PEAR · Fruit Basket No. 10
DS and DSi emulator. Say it out loud: a pair of screens. Narrow over wide, hinged
at the waist. A 16 x 16 sprite, shown only at whole-number scales.

SPRITE COLOURS (15-bit safe)
  Cream F0E8D8   Shade B8B0A0   Ink 181818   Red D03828

CHROME COLOURS
  Paper F4EFE3   Ink 1A1A1A    Seed C8322A
  Night 141414   Cream EDE6D6  Night seed E5483C

WORDMARK
  Archivo ExtraBold (wght 800) at 72% width (wdth 72), capitals.
  Text in the SVG wordmarks and lockups is outlined, so no font is needed.
  Over a game, use Silkscreen at multiples of 8 px (see the in-game panel).

USE
  Scale the sprite by whole numbers only: 1x, 2x, 3x. Never smooth it.
  The lockup SVG puts one sprite pixel on 6 units, so render it at 100%, 200%
  and so on to keep it crisp.
  -night files are for dark backgrounds.
  The mark shows only where no game is: app icon, site, empty library.
